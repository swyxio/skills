# Podcast promotion on X: examples and inspiration

Observed October 20, 2026. This is a small, selectively researched inspiration
bank, not a representative sample or a ranking experiment. Counts are visible
post views, likes, and bookmarks at that time, not unique viewers, episode
plays, or conversions. Account size, post age, topic, format, and distribution
differ. Refresh before relying on them. Timestamps in a native X video refer to
that uploaded video; verify against the released edit before creating your own.

## From accounts verified on swyx's following list

- [Aakash Gupta: an AI startup's operating system](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2069873461636558856) (June 24, 2026): a concrete result, a full 1:07:53 native episode, and clickable timestamps spelling out what to learn; YouTube is in a [reply](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2069877310648443295). 388,766 views, 677 likes, 2,038 bookmarks. **Try:** an unusually useful chapter menu. **Tradeoff:** the initial hook does not name the guest, and putting the destination in a reply can add friction; this example does not prove it increases reach.
- [Aakash: adversarial agents in Claude Code](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2077470028480549202) (July 15, 2026): leads with a demonstrable outcome, then specific learning chapters and native video. 167,187 views, 881 likes, 2,183 bookmarks. **Try:** promise a practical result and let the chapter list demonstrate depth. **Tradeoff:** verify the characterization and ensure the episode really delivers the stated result.
- [Aakash: basics of technical AI](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2080406855734657174) (July 23, 2026): a beginner-oriented promise followed by granular, time-linked lessons. 76,255 views, 347 likes, 788 bookmarks. **Try:** a curriculum-shaped post when the interview teaches a coherent sequence. **Tradeoff:** a long list can bury the most interesting idea.
- [Aakash: Zapier and n8n CEOs](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2108574276458828225) (October 9, 2026): uses an existing rivalry as setup for a 2:03 native clip and connects it to a prior episode. 4,492 views, 8 likes, 3 bookmarks. **Try:** use a real tension and a callback to a past conversation. **Tradeoff:** a rivalry can manufacture drama if the clip does not support it; this example had modest visible engagement.
- [Ian Livingstone: the modern data stack](https://x.com/ianlivingstone/status/1677036551891566592) (July 6, 2023): opens on a forward-looking question, names the guest, then offers concrete topics with a Spotify card. 8,148 views, 36 likes, 11 bookmarks. **Try:** a sharp question plus guest expertise and a direct listening link. **Tradeoff:** generic questions and topic lists can sound interchangeable across episodes.
- [Ian: OpenAI Agent SDK and CrewAI](https://x.com/ianlivingstone/status/1899836900405629178) (March 12, 2025): connects a current announcement to a guest's relevant experience and quotes the show's announcement. 5,499 views, 21 likes, 3 bookmarks. **Try:** a timely news peg when the episode genuinely helps explain it. **Tradeoff:** the peg can date quickly or overshadow the guest's own insight.
- [Ian: infrastructure from code](https://x.com/ianlivingstone/status/1740096339361276168) (December 27, 2023): explains a technical concept through a guest conversation and a direct listening link. 5,246 views, 21 likes, 10 bookmarks. **Try:** frame the unfamiliar term as a question the episode answers. **Tradeoff:** add a concrete consequence so it doesn't read like a glossary entry.
- [ThePrimeagen: DHH interview](https://x.com/ThePrimeagen/status/1836380882451550587) (September 18, 2024): exceptionally short guest-led announcement with a YouTube preview. 240,124 views, 937 likes, 264 bookmarks. **Try:** minimal copy when the guest or existing relationship is itself the draw. **Tradeoff:** strong existing audience and guest recognition may do much of the work; it supplies little context to a stranger.
- [Aakash: podcast launch story](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2109350004909559877) (October 11, 2026): frames a new show through a founder-and-media-business story, quoting its launch. 39,760 views, 155 likes, 229 bookmarks. **Paid partnership.** **Try:** explain why the show exists and why its hosts have a distinctive perspective. **Tradeoff:** this is sponsored, so don't treat its distribution as organic evidence; the analogy must fit the actual show.

## Latent Space and its network

These are first-party posts or accounts returned by the Latent Space account's
`filter:follows`; they were not independently verified as swyx personal follows.

- [Thariq's episode clip thread](https://x.com/trq212/status/2104983034152063339) (September 29, 2026): guest introduces a series of favorite clips and gives the first a standalone technical thesis. 48,920 views, 354 likes, 184 bookmarks. **Try:** supply guests with a clip and an optional point they can express in their own voice. **Tradeoff:** a guest thread depends on guest participation; don't post in their name.
- [Latent Space: guest insight and clip](https://x.com/latentspacepod/status/2110471907862618502) (October 14, 2026): one concrete finding and a 1:23 native clip. 3,132 views, 24 likes, 8 bookmarks. **Try:** make a single argument understandable before the viewer opens the episode. **Tradeoff:** a clip that needs too much context will lose the point.
- [Latent Space: High Taste Testers launch](https://x.com/latentspacepod/status/2111244257579409664) (October 16, 2026): series identity first, then format, guests and specific topics, with the full native video. 32,930 views, 99 likes, 78 bookmarks. **Try:** make the show's recurring promise explicit for a series launch. **Tradeoff:** too many names and topics dilute the opening; a full upload may not serve a YouTube click goal.
- [Latent Space: Jev clip and callback](https://x.com/latentspacepod/status/2103141406583722375) (September 24, 2026): a secondary clip quotes an earlier episode announcement. 16,181 views, 57 likes, 21 bookmarks. **Try:** revisit a strong idea later with a fresh, self-contained clip. **Tradeoff:** give enough context that the callback is accessible to new readers.
- [Emmanuel Ameisen: guest question and thread](https://x.com/mlpowered/status/2111921617815421185) (October 18, 2026): frames interpretability as an answer to a basic question, quotes the host's announcement, then shares takeaways in a thread. 7,219 views, 46 likes, 32 bookmarks. **Try:** turn a complex interview into a question and a short sequence of insights. **Tradeoff:** readers may not open every reply; the lead should already convey value.
- [Yenkel: why we made this show](https://x.com/yenkel/status/2112341171020120231) (October 19, 2026): founders explain how an existing conversation became a LATAM technology show, with a 1:44 native video. 5,375 views, 39 likes, 8 bookmarks. **Try:** a human origin story for a new series. **Tradeoff:** make the audience benefit clear, not just the hosts' enthusiasm.

## How to use these

For each episode, offer distinct alternatives such as a surprising thesis and
clip, a practical payoff and timestamp menu, a timely peg, and a guest-led or
series-led announcement. Recommend one, argue against each, and cite the
episode moments behind the claims. Pair the format with the objective: native
viewing, engagement, newsletter visits, or full-episode listens. Compare actual
outcomes where available; visible X views alone cannot tell which drove listens.

# Encoded-result review

Sample settled and near-exit frames for every cue from the final encoded deliverable. Add frames at camera cuts and points of emphasis. Play moving inserts and transitions with their audio; sample untouched footage for continuity. Contact sheets establish coverage, not audiovisual quality.

Record cue/time, planned visual, output/source evidence, and keep/refine/replace/remove disposition. Check:

- Selected asset actually appears; no unreported omission or text substitution.
- Useful detail is readable within the hold; the active speaker stays clear.
- Highlights have padding and remain aligned during motion.
- Clips show the relevant action/result; repeated clips add something new.
- The visual explains the actual point at that moment: diagrams make relationships legible, paired sources appear in a meaningful sequence, and pull quotes match the speaker's words and timing.
- The insert answers its viewer question during the dialogue; generated illustrations read as illustrative and do not stand in for real evidence.
- Attribution is useful and concise; no distracting boilerplate or player chrome.
- Entrances/exits and sound cues align without masking speech.

Fix shared treatment defects once, then check affected cues. Technical duration/decode/audio checks complement editorial review. Say what was inspected and played; do not claim continuous playback from sampled frames. Source recovery is not a corrected render, and a local render is not a replaced upload.

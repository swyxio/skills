# Review the encoded result

Use the delivered encode and its timebase. For a single fix, check the changed
passage and its joins. For a preview, inspect the question the excerpt was
built to answer. For a full render, inspect a settled and near-exit frame of
every cue, add samples at camera cuts and points of emphasis, play moving
inserts and transitions with their audio, and sample untouched conversation.

## Check the picture and the dialogue

- Match each cue to the selected asset and action. On a combined sequence,
  inspect a settled frame from each treatment and play the handoff, including
  a second beat placed later. When a revision sits beside an explicit Keep,
  sample that kept shot too.
- Inspect faces, meaningful reactions, cursor targets, source labels, chart
  axes, captions and anything the guest points to. Check the whole graphic,
  including its gradient and entrance path. Sample each distinct layout and
  risky camera cut at full size and phone size. Move, reflow, shorten or omit
  an overlay that obscures the point.
- Compare annotated figures with the source in both wide and close views.
  Follow the highlight through the move. Check complete quotations, speaker
  attribution, reading time and the alignment of emphasis with speech.
- Play reveals and exits at normal speed with narration. Check that each
  number lands on its spoken cue, a referenced episode appears when named, and
  each selected demo plays through the action viewers need to see. For a puzzle
  or mechanism, check that the setup lands before its consequence. Allow time
  to see a result before cutting away. On a PiP takeover, play the shrink, hold
  and return to check framing and lip sync.
- If the mix changed, listen across the joins at normal volume and check true
  peak. Effects should leave dialogue intelligible. If the mix is the only
  correction, remux the new audio with the existing picture.

## Listen to the exported intro

Before releasing a Latent Space episode with an edited host intro, listen to
the final export once at normal speed from the cold open through the first
interview line. Listen for abandoned takes, repeated pickups, distracting ums
and uhs, claps, clicks, abrupt joins, and rough handoffs from music to dialogue.
If the podcast and video use different intro cuts or mixes, listen to both.

Fix any issues, re-export, and replay the repaired joins. Record the export
version and playback range in the review. Use the transcript and loudness
measurements to help locate issues. If playback is unavailable, mark the
listening check as pending before release.

## When the edit uses a chapter rail

Check the current label and its neighbors across a chapter handover. Derive the
fill from `(episode time - chapter start) / chapter duration`, clamped to 0–1.
Use chapter times from the final cut and the episode end for the last chapter.
Sample a mid-chapter preview and the return from a clean full-screen shot.
The bar should resume at the elapsed position in both cases.

On a Latent Space edit, compare the encode with the [house style](../SKILL.md#latent-space-house-style).
Check the logo across camera and takeover cuts, the progress bars, and quote
contrast on bright and dark shots. During screen shares, check that the share
takes over the frame and its labels and cursor stay visible around the lower
third and speaker insets.

For a standalone host intro, play the side overlays through the host's movement
and any jump cuts. At phone size, check that graphics sit beside the host,
leave the bottom of the frame clear for captions, and give viewers time to read
key figures and URLs. Play the handoff through the show opener and theme into
the first interview line.

## Hand off

Record the cue or timestamp, what was observed, and any correction or omission.
Check other uses of a corrected shared treatment within scope. Report which
portions were sampled or played and where playback was unavailable. Extracted
frames support a framing decision. Timing, transitions and audio need playback.

# Learned Thumbnail Preferences

## Editorial objective

The thumbnail should sell one concrete reason to click, not summarize the episode. Prefer a surprising consequence, unresolved decision, sharp contrast, credible question, or future capability expressed as action.

- Use one curiosity gap and one dominant headline.
- Favor two to six words; extend only when the phrase remains instantly legible on a phone.
- Prefer specific hooks such as `VOICE QUALITY IS SOLVED` or `OUTBOUND BEATS INBOUND?` over broad labels such as `THE FUTURE OF VOICE AI`.
- Use numbers only when the episode supports them and they improve the hook.
- Do not add explanatory subcopy to rescue a weak headline.

## Visual system

- Canvas: 16:9, normally a higher-resolution master plus 1280 x 720 delivery JPG.
- Base: near-black or deep violet.
- Type: white with one emphasis color, usually acid lime; restrained lavender is the secondary accent. Amber may support a directional or warning concept.
- Composition: one hero area, a compact remaining-cast strip when needed, and one simple motif such as a waveform, fork, arrow, or score line.
- Branding: official Latent Space purple-gradient hex plus `FDE` in the upper-right. Keep the lockup compact and clear of faces.
- Typography: Tachyon is preferred for FDE branding when the real font asset exists. If it does not, use a clean futuristic geometric fallback and do not claim exact Tachyon fidelity.

## People and company coverage

- Show every requested guest exactly once and no extra host unless requested.
- Use supplied photos for recognizable identity. Treat generated likenesses as a fidelity risk requiring inspection.
- Show every participating company exactly once in a simple readable rail. Plain exact typesetting is better than a hallucinated logo.
- Company coverage is content, not decoration. Do not shrink or drop a company to simplify the layout.

## YouTube duration-overlay safety

Reserve the lower-right corner for YouTube's duration badge. At 1280 x 720, keep essential content out of approximately:

- `x = 1050–1279` — rightmost 18 percent
- `y = 620–719` — bottom 14 percent

Scale this exclusion zone proportionally for other resolutions. Background color may continue through it, but no wordmark, face, headline, or other essential element may enter it. In a company rail, place DAILY immediately after the preceding company and terminate the rail before the safe zone.

## Wardrobe and silhouette separation

Near-black clothing can erase the cast against the house background.

- Give every portrait a readable silhouette through lighter wardrobe, edge lighting, or a restrained glow.
- Avoid clustering several adjacent black tops.
- When the hero and multiple central panelists wear dark clothing, recolor the hero to cream or warm gray and the smaller dark tops to pale lavender, cool gray, or soft off-white.
- Preserve faces, hair, skin tone, pose, and identity while changing clothing.
- Keep existing orange, coral, or other useful midtone clothing when it already separates cleanly.

## Multiple directions

Vary the idea and layout substantially. Useful mechanisms include:

- decision split;
- solved-problem provocation;
- directional bet;
- evaluation question;
- single surprising production constraint.

Do not return palette swaps or the same hero arrangement four times.

## QA checklist

Inspect both the full-size image and a 320 x 180 preview:

- headline is letter-for-letter correct;
- one clear idea survives at mobile size;
- every requested person appears once;
- every requested company appears once;
- faces and hands are natural;
- official LS logo is intact;
- FDE lockup is readable;
- clothing separates from the background;
- bottom-right duration zone is empty;
- the company rail is readable and not cropped.

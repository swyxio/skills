# Example Index

The examples are approved for overall direction and production discipline. They are not interchangeable episode templates.

## `prompts-vs-workflows-safe.jpg`

- Mechanism: decision split.
- Strength: clean 60/40 hierarchy, large readable contrast, recognizable turquoise-phone hero, restrained fork motif.
- Production lesson: light hero and central shirts separate cleanly from the black field; the company rail ends before the timestamp zone.

## `voice-quality-is-solved-safe.jpg`

- Mechanism: solved-problem provocation.
- Strength: broadest hook, simplest waveform, strongest general-audience option.
- Production lesson: one dominant face and a compact cast strip can preserve complete coverage without feeling busy.

## `outbound-beats-inbound-safe.jpg`

- Mechanism: directional bet.
- Strength: amber emphasis and opposing call arrows turn a technical claim into an immediate visual argument.
- Production lesson: a secondary accent is useful when it carries meaning rather than merely changing the palette.

## `how-do-you-eval-a-voice-safe.jpg`

- Mechanism: evaluation question.
- Strength: technical curiosity with a precision-waveform motif.
- Production lesson: lavender can label a key verb while acid lime carries the object of curiosity.

## Supporting references

- `wayfinder-sparse.png` demonstrates negative space, concise series branding, and a single portrait with excellent silhouette separation.
- `stripe-for-agents-legacy.png` demonstrates oversized faces and emphatic headline scale, but is intentionally busier than the current preferred FDE system.

## Known failure patterns

- Putting DAILY at the extreme bottom-right causes the YouTube duration badge to cover it.
- A dark-clothed hero plus multiple dark-clothed central panelists collapses into the background.
- Dropping a person or company to obtain a cleaner composition is unacceptable when complete coverage was requested.
- Four directions with the same structure and different colors are not meaningfully different directions.
- Broad topic labels and screenshot collages create information density without a strong curiosity gap.
